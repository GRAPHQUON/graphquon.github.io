GRAPHQUON 2026

McGill University, Montreal, Quebec, October 23-25, 2026

Location McConnell Building, Room TBA
Registration Deadline TBA

About

GRAPHQUON is the annual Quebec–Ontario pre-SIGGRAPH workshop, organized by and for computer graphics research labs across the region. The workshop provides students and researchers a friendly, constructively critical venue to present ongoing work and prospective submissions, receive valuable feedback prior to paper deadlines, and build lasting cross-institutional connections.

The date of the workshop is earlier than previous editions to permit more time for participants to improve their work before submission based on the feedback received. Social activities will be planned for those arriving in time in the afternoon or evening of Friday October 23. The workshop will likewise end early afternoon of Sunday, October 25, to accommodate travel plans.

Program

The program for GRAPHQUON 2026 will include keynotes, student talks, and other activities. A preliminary schedule will be available soon, and a final schedule with assigned presentation slots will be posted after registraiton closes. Note that titles and abstracts will not be posted on the web so as to preserve anonymity for works that are about to be submitted.

Keynotes

Keynote speakers for 2026 will be announced soon. We aim to feature speakers spanning animation, rendering, simulation, geometry processing, and machine learning for graphics.
