About

GRAPHQUON is the annual Quebec–Ontario pre-SIGGRAPH workshop, organized by and for computer graphics research labs across the region. The workshop provides students and researchers a friendly, constructively critical venue to present ongoing work and prospective submissions, receive valuable feedback prior to paper deadlines, and build lasting cross-institutional connections.

The date of the workshop is earlier than previous editions to permit more time for participants to improve their work before submission based on the feedback received. Social activities will be planned for those arriving in the afternoon or evening of Friday October 23. The workshop will likewise end early afternoon of Sunday, October 25, to accommodate travel plans.

Keynote

J.P. Lewis is a numerical programmer and researcher working in machine learning. He has worked as as a research scientist at NVIDIA Research and Google Research, and also worked applying ML and computer vision in the movie and games industries, including at Weta Digital, ILM, and ESC, and at Electronic Arts where he served as principal technical director. He received credits on a few movies including Avatar and The Matrix sequels, and his algorithms have been adopted in commercial software including Maya, Matlab, and several game engines. Lewis is also an adjunct Associate Professor in the machine learning group of Victoria University. His research has been published in peer-reviewed venues including ACM TOG/Siggraph, AAAI, ECCV, ICML, and CVPR.
